# Summary of "Meta’s Generative Ads Model (GEM): The Central Brain Accelerating Ads Recommendation AI Innovation"

- **Purpose and impact**: GEM is Meta's large-scale generative ads recommendation foundation model designed to boost downstream ad-ranking systems. It has already delivered a 5% lift in Instagram ad conversions and 3% in Facebook Feed conversions by providing more accurate, personalized recommendations.
- **Architectural advances**: GEM mixes custom attention for sequence and non-sequence features, stackable factorization machines (Wukong blocks) for user–ad attribute interactions, and the InterFormer design for interleaving sequence modeling with cross-feature learning. These innovations enable 4× more efficient performance gains per unit of data and compute than prior models while handling long user-behavior histories.
- **Knowledge transfer**: After training, GEM shares its knowledge with hundreds of vertical models via direct and hierarchical transfer. Techniques such as student adapters, representation learning, and selective parameter sharing double the effectiveness of standard knowledge distillation, helping smaller models inherit GEM’s richer signals without incurring its inference cost.
- **Training stack**: The model trains on thousands of GPUs using multi-dimensional parallelism, custom GPU kernels for jagged sequences, FP8 activation compression, and NCCLX communication collectives. These optimizations deliver a 23× increase in effective training FLOPs and a 1.43× improvement in MFU while reducing job startup time fivefold.
- **Future direction**: Meta plans to extend GEM across all Facebook and Instagram surfaces, incorporate multimodal organic and ad data (text, images, audio, video), and explore inference-time scaling plus agentic advertiser tools to keep improving return on ad spend.
